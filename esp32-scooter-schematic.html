XIAO Scooter Control Board

Build notes

Power: two ways in

Cleanest is a 41 V→5 V buck off the pack into the XIAO's 5 V pin — it doesn't load the controller and leaves room for a standby cutoff later. Or tap the controller's own 5 V rail if it can spare ~100 mA for MCU + OLED + sensors. Tie all grounds together.

Sensors need 5 V

The Hall throttle and brake are 5 V parts. Feed their VCC from the 5 V rail, run the signal back through the dividers. Calibrate idle and full-scale in software and average a few samples — the C3 ADC is a touch noisy.

Brake logic lives in firmware

The controller has no brake field. Mirror the stock board: when the brake ADC crosses ~half travel, force the throttle byte in the 0x1A poll to 0x00. Braking is "command speed zero", nothing more on the wire.

Why not just a bare divider on TX?

The bus is bidirectional. TX must release the line so the controller can drive its replies — that's what open-drain gives you. A plain divider or a diode can't reach a low the controller reads against its stiff 740 Ω pull-up; the 100 Ω hack proved that on the bench.
